Before Junoon Wellness has a member to onboard or a client to coach, it needs someone to land on a page and sign up — that's what this site is for. Built with Next.js and Framer Motion for the animation work, it's a product-first waitlist page showing a live subscriber count pulled from Beehiiv, plus contact, privacy, and terms pages rounding out the basics. The contact form skips third-party form services entirely and sends mail directly through Nodemailer. It's the simplest of the three Junoon sites by design — no auth, no payments, no dashboard, just the job of getting someone from curious to signed up.
