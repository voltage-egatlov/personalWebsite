Junoon Wellness's coaching business needed its own front door, distinct from the main subscription app's marketing pages. Junoon Coaching is that front door: a Next.js (App Router) site covering coach bios, a shop, testimonials, a blog with individual post pages, and a contact flow, styled with Tailwind CSS v4 and Geist. It's built as a proper component library rather than one-off pages — shared Nav, Footer, Gallery, PageHero, and DashList components — so the site reads as a cohesive product rather than a template stitched together, without carrying any of the backend complexity (auth, payments, video) the main platform needs.
